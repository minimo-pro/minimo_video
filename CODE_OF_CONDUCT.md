# Code of Conduct

We want **minimo (video)** to be a welcoming project for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback and different points of view.
- Respect other people's privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sharing private information without permission are not accepted.

## Reporting a problem

Report unacceptable behavior privately to [khlebobul@gmail.com](mailto:khlebobul@gmail.com). Include relevant links or screenshots when possible.

Reports will be reviewed confidentially. Project maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban participants who violate these rules.
